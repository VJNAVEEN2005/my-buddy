01 · Point and ask

Ask.
It points.

Stuck in an app again? Put your mouse on it and ask. Nova looks at your screen, flies over to the right button and circles it, then tells you what to click.

Ctrl+Shift+Y on any app
02 · Voice

Or just talk.

Hold two keys and speak, or turn on the wake word and say “Hey Buddy”. Nova talks back in a real voice.

Your speech is turned into text on your own PC, by a speech model that never leaves it.

Hold Ctrl+Alt to talk · tap Ctrl+Shift to type
03 · Errands in your Chrome

It does the clicks. And asks before it sends.

With the My Buddy extension on, it works your own signed-in Chrome in a background tab: send an email with your notes, fill a form, cancel a subscription.

Anything risky, like send, pay, cancel or delete, waits for your okay. It never types passwords or card numbers.

04 · Explains it

Draws it out for you.

Ask how a cell works, when World War II turned, or how to solve a quadratic. Nova sketches cards right on your desktop: diagrams, timelines, maps, graphs and quick quizzes.

05 · Study

Exams? It keeps you on track.

A focus timer that notices when you drift, a daily streak, flashcards that come back on the right day, and quizzes made from your own notes.

Preparing for SSC, UPSC or GATE? Get a daily plan by subject, revision reminders, mock-test scores and a countdown badge to exam day.

06 · Memory

It remembers, so you don’t have to.

Tell it once: “remind me every weekday at 9”. It keeps your commitments and what matters to you, and greets you with the day ahead.

Yours, not ours

Memory stays on your PC. Any AI, even a local one. No tracking.

Bring your own model

Sign in with Claude or ChatGPT, use an API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, OpenRouter), or run a local model with Ollama or LM Studio.

Only where you send it

What you ask, and a screenshot when you ask about your screen, goes only to the AI you picked. Memory and settings stay on your computer.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes.

Download

Grab the installer for Windows 10 or 11. It installs just for you, no admin needed.

Run it

If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click More info, then Run anyway. Signing is on its way.

Say hi

Pick your AI, choose a buddy, and hold Ctrl+Alt to say hello.
